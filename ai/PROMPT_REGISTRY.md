# AI Prompt Registry

## Versioning

Use:
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH

MAJOR:
architectural or behavioral contract change.

MINOR:
new compatible requirement.

PATCH:
wording/clarification with no semantic engineering change.

Prompts are immutable after issuance.
